<h2>Why paying extra makes such a difference</h2>
<p>
  Interest is charged on your outstanding balance. Every extra rand you pay reduces that
  balance immediately, so it reduces the interest charged for every remaining month of the
  loan. Even a modest additional amount paid consistently can shorten a 20-year bond
  meaningfully.
</p>

<h2>Before you commit to extra payments</h2>
<p>
  Check the terms of your specific home loan with your bank, including how additional
  payments are applied and whether you can access those funds again if you need them.
</p>
