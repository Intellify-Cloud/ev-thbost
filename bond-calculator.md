<h2>How your repayment is worked out</h2>
<p>
  Your repayment depends on three things: the amount you borrow, the interest rate the bank
  offers you, and the term of the loan (usually 20 years). A higher deposit reduces the
  amount you borrow, and a lower interest rate reduces what you pay over the full term.
</p>

<h2>Before you rely on this figure</h2>
<p>
  This calculator gives an estimate for planning purposes. It is not a quotation or an offer
  of credit. Your actual rate is set by the bank after it assesses your credit record,
  income and affordability. Remember to also budget for bond registration and transfer
  costs, rates, levies and home insurance.
</p>
