<h2>Why the early years are mostly interest</h2>
<p>
  In the first years of a bond, most of your repayment goes towards interest because the
  outstanding balance is still high. As the balance drops, a larger share of each repayment
  goes towards paying off the capital. This is why paying extra early in the term has a much
  bigger effect than paying extra near the end.
</p>
