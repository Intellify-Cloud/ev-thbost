<h2>What affects how much you qualify for</h2>
<p>
  Banks look at your gross monthly income, your existing debt repayments, your living
  expenses, your credit record and the size of your deposit. Reducing short-term debt and
  keeping your credit record clean before you apply generally improves the amount and the
  rate a bank is willing to offer.
</p>

<h2>This is an estimate, not an approval</h2>
<p>
  The result is a guide to help you plan your property search. Each bank applies its own
  affordability and credit criteria, so the amount you are actually approved for may differ.
  The only way to know what a bank will offer is to submit an application.
</p>
