<h2>What these costs cover</h2>
<p>
  <strong>Bond registration costs</strong> are the attorney fees and charges for registering
  the home loan over the property at the Deeds Office.
  <strong>Transfer costs</strong> cover the conveyancing attorney who transfers ownership
  into your name.
  <strong>Transfer duty</strong> is a government tax based on the purchase price, and
  lower-value properties may fall below the threshold where it applies.
</p>

<h2>Budgeting for these costs</h2>
<p>
  These amounts are usually payable before registration and are generally not included in
  your home loan, so they need to be budgeted separately from your deposit. Figures shown
  are estimates and the final amounts are confirmed by the attorneys handling the transaction.
</p>
