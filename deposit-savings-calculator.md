<h2>Why a deposit helps</h2>
<p>
  A deposit reduces the amount you need to borrow, which lowers your monthly repayment. It
  also signals lower risk to the bank, which can improve the interest rate you are offered.
  It is possible to apply for a 100% home loan, but a deposit generally strengthens your
  application.
</p>
